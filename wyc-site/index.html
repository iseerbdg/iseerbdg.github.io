Racing at WYC

Big-fleet energy.
Lake-level access.

WYC oversees one of the largest sailboat racing programs in the United States, with more than 65 Lake Minnetonka keelboat races each season and evening participation reaching 130 boats.

From tactical one-design racing to PHRF and MORC handicap fleets, there is a starting line for nearly every kind of sailor.

About Wayzata Yacht Club

Competitive by nature.
Welcoming by choice.

Founded in 1965 by 15 sailors, WYC grew by putting racing and community ahead of exclusivity. Today, the club brings together more than 600 members across Lake Minnetonka and Lake Superior.

It remains a member-owned, working sailing club: serious about good race management, generous with knowledge, and always looking for the next person who wants to sail.
